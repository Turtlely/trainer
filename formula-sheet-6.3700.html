Side 2 — L12–L13

Covariance

$\operatorname{cov}(X,Y)=E[XY]-EX\,EY$

$\operatorname{cov}(aX+b,cY+d)=ac\operatorname{cov}(X,Y)$, bilinear

$\operatorname{Var}(X+Y)=\operatorname{Var}X+\operatorname{Var}Y+2\operatorname{cov}(X,Y)$

$\operatorname{Var}\sum X_i=\sum\operatorname{Var}X_i+\sum_{i\ne j}\operatorname{cov}(X_i,X_j)$

$\rho=\frac{\operatorname{cov}(X,Y)}{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}\in[-1,1]$; $|\rho|=1$ iff linear

indep $\Rightarrow$ cov $=0$ (not conversely: $X\sim U[-1,1]$, $Y=X^2$)

$\operatorname{cov}(I_A,I_B)=P(A\cap B)-P(A)P(B)$

indep: $\operatorname{Var}(XY)=E[X^2]E[Y^2]-(EX\,EY)^2$

Conditional expectation

$E[X\mid Y]$ is a r.v. (a function of $Y$)

$E\big[E[X\mid Y]\big]=E[X]$

$\operatorname{Var}X=E[\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y)]+\operatorname{Var}(E[X\mid Y])$

Random sum $Y=\sum_{i=1}^N X_i$:

$\quad E Y=EN\,EX$, $\;\operatorname{Var}Y=EN\operatorname{Var}X+(EX)^2\operatorname{Var}N$
