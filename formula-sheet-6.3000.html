6.3000 Quiz 2 — formulas to copy onto your handwritten sheets (6.3000 conventions: $\frac1T$, $\frac1N$ in analysis)

Four Fourier representations

analysissynthesis
CTFS$a_k=\frac1T\int_T f(t)e^{-jk\omega_0t}dt$$f(t)=\sum_k a_ke^{jk\omega_0t}$
DTFS$a_k=\frac1N\sum_{n=\langle N\rangle}f[n]e^{-jk\Omega_0n}$$f[n]=\sum_{k=\langle N\rangle}a_ke^{jk\Omega_0n}$
CTFT$X(\omega)=\int x(t)e^{-j\omega t}dt$$x(t)=\frac1{2\pi}\int X(\omega)e^{j\omega t}d\omega$
DTFT$X(\Omega)=\sum_n x[n]e^{-j\Omega n}$$x[n]=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{2\pi}X(\Omega)e^{j\Omega n}d\Omega$
DFT$X[k]=\frac1N\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}x[n]e^{-j\frac{2\pi kn}N}$$x[n]=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}X[k]e^{j\frac{2\pi kn}N}$

$\omega_0=\frac{2\pi}T$, $\Omega_0=\frac{2\pi}N$. Trig form: $c_0=a_0$, $c_k=2\operatorname{Re}a_k$, $d_k=-2\operatorname{Im}a_k$.

DFT = $\frac1N\times$ DTFT samples: $X[k]=\frac1NX(2\pi k/N)$. $X[0]$ = average.

Pairs

$\delta(t)\leftrightarrow1$, $\;1\leftrightarrow2\pi\delta(\omega)$, $\;e^{j\omega_0t}\leftrightarrow2\pi\delta(\omega-\omega_0)$

$e^{-at}u(t)\leftrightarrow\frac1{a+j\omega}$, $\;\;a^nu[n]\leftrightarrow\frac1{1-ae^{-j\Omega}}$ ($|a|<1$)

pulse $|t|<T_1$: $\frac{2\sin\omega T_1}{\omega}$; triangle $1-|t|$: $\left(\frac{\sin\omega/2}{\omega/2}\right)^2$

ideal LPF $|\omega|<W$: $\frac{\sin Wt}{\pi t}$; DT pulse $0..L-1$: $e^{-j\Omega\frac{L-1}2}\frac{\sin(L\Omega/2)}{\sin(\Omega/2)}$

periodic $f$: $X(\omega)=\sum_k2\pi a_k\delta(\omega-k\omega_0)$

square duty $D$ centered: $a_k=\frac{\sin\pi kD}{\pi k}$; $\pm1$ square: $\frac2{j\pi k}$ odd $k$

Sums and identities

$\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}z^n=\frac{1-z^N}{1-z}$, $\;\sum_{n\ge0}z^n=\frac1{1-z}$

$\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}e^{-j2\pi mn/N}=N$ if $N\mid m$, else $0$

$\cos\theta=\frac{e^{j\theta}+e^{-j\theta}}2$, $\sin\theta=\frac{e^{j\theta}-e^{-j\theta}}{2j}$, $\;1-e^{-j2\theta}=2je^{-j\theta}\sin\theta$

$\cos^2=\frac12+\frac12\cos2\theta$, $\;\cos^3=\frac34\cos\theta+\frac14\cos3\theta$

Sampling

$x[n]=x(nT_s)$, $\Omega=\omega T_s=2\pi f/f_s$; DT freq only mod $2\pi$

Nyquist: $f_s>2f_{max}$; $f$ aliases to $|f-mf_s|$ in $[0,f_s/2]$

$\cos(2\pi pn/q)$ period $q/\gcd(p,q)$

Systems

additive: $x_1+x_2\to y_1+y_2$; homogeneous: $cx\to cy$ (try $c=-1, 0$); TI: $x[n-n_0]\to y[n-n_0]$

red flags: constants ($+1$), $x^2$, $|x|$, $\sin x$ (nonlinear); explicit $n$, $x[-n]$, $x[2n]$, $x\cos\omega t$ (time-varying)

LTI: $y=x*h$; cascade $h_1*h_2$, $H_1H_2$; parallel $h_1+h_2$

$\sum a_ky[n-k]=\sum b_kx[n-k]\Rightarrow H(\Omega)=\frac{\sum b_ke^{-jk\Omega}}{\sum a_ke^{-jk\Omega}}$

$y+\tau y'=x\Rightarrow H=\frac1{1+j\omega\tau}$

Convolution

$(x*h)[n]=\sum_kx[k]h[n-k]$: add shifted copies of $x$ scaled by $h[k]$

support: starts add, ends add; length $L_1+L_2-1$; sums (areas) multiply

$u*u=(n+1)u[n]$, $\;u*u*u=\frac{(n+1)(n+2)}2$, $\;a^nu*u=\frac{1-a^{n+1}}{1-a}$

circular $N$: linear conv, then wrap mod $N$ (no wrap if $N\ge L_1+L_2-1$)

Frequency response

$e^{j\Omega n}\to H(\Omega)e^{j\Omega n}$; $\cos\Omega_0n\to|H|\cos(\Omega_0n+\angle H)$

$=\operatorname{Re}H\cos\Omega_0n-\operatorname{Im}H\sin\Omega_0n$

$H(0)=\sum h$, $H(\pi)=\sum(-1)^nh[n]$; linear angle slope $-d$ = delay $d$; jumps of $\pi$ = sign changes

Modulation (AM)

$y=x\cos\omega_ct$: copies at $\pm\omega_c$, band $\omega_c\pm W$; need $\omega_c>W$

demod: $\times\cos\omega_ct$, LPF gain 2, cutoff in $(W,2\omega_c-W)$; phase error $\theta\Rightarrow\times\cos\theta$

DFT practice

bin $k$ at $f=kf_s/N$; spacing $f_s/N$; window $N/f_s$ s

real cos on bin $m$: spikes $\frac12$ at $k=m,N-m$; off-bin: leakage

$(-1)^nx[n]\to X[k-\frac N2]$; zero padding = denser samples, same resolution

main lobe of length-$N$ window: $\pm\frac{2\pi}N$
